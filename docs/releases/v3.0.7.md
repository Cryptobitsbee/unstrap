A bug-fix release, from a careful outside check of 3.0.5. Workouts are safer, badges and bests come without a workout, and a few screens that showed too little now show it.

## Workouts

- **A workout left recording is never deleted for looking short.** One left paused comes back paused, and a GPS run with no heart rate ends at its last route point.
- A rest in a strength workout never ends while the workout is paused.
- **A workout the strap holds but unstrap can't read no longer stops workout syncing.** It is skipped and the next ones still come. A heart rate or step chunk that can't be read now fails its type, so it is tried again.
- A phone workout's distance keeps the steps walked before GPS found its first fix, instead of dropping when it does.
- A trimmed or untrimmed workout works its fastest 1k, 5k and 10k out again from the route it keeps.

## Badges and bests

- **Step badges and bests no longer wait for a workout.** They follow each sync, and deleting the last workout takes its bests with it.
- The rewards screen tries its read again, instead of staying empty.

## Health Connect

- **A deleted phone workout is taken out of Health Connect too,** with its session and distance.

## Sleep

- Sleep edits that match no sleep are cleared, so one left behind by a deleted minute night can't move or hide a real night. Only main sleeps in the weeks minute nights were made for are touched.
- The oldest of the fourteen nights averages its HRV, skin temperature, blood oxygen and breathing from the evening it began.

## Smaller things

- **Your week, opened from a past day, shows the days after it:** their active minutes and sleep.
- Alarms: a failed read no longer hides the health switches (and the reverse), and a failed write keeps its reason on screen.
- A calorie goal the strap refused is tried again on the next connect, not marked as sent.
- Auto backup keeps the day's earlier backup until the new one is in place.
- Healthspan says it updates weekly, not "next in 0 days", and the weekly recap's streak line only shows on a week that met the goal.

## Upgrading

Installs over 3.0.5 and keeps all your data. The database moves to version 32 on first start. Android 11 or newer.
