A reliability release. Workouts, syncing and the connection to the strap hold up better when something goes wrong, and vitals show the readings the strap took.

## Vitals

- **Skin temperature syncs again.** 3.0.3 stopped asking the strap for it. It leads with the measured value now, with the change from normal under it.
- **Blood oxygen and stress show the latest reading**, not the day's average. Blood oxygen says whether it was taken in the day or while asleep, and readings outside 80 to 100 are left out, as Zepp does.
- **Stress is judged by the reading shown**, using Zepp's relaxed, normal, medium and high bands.
- HRV, resting heart rate and heart rate show the same number on every screen.

## Health Connect

- **Breathing rate, VO2 max and strap workouts are exported.** Health Connect asks for two new permissions for these.
- Every blood oxygen reading is exported, not one average.

## Workouts

- **A workout that fails to save says so** and offers Try again, instead of losing it.
- If ending a workout doesn't reach the strap, it's sent again on the next connect, so the strap stops recording.
- A restarted workout keeps its added rest time and live calories.
- Workout notifications follow the workout, and Discard can't leave half a workout behind.

## Connection and sync

- **Bluetooth off has its own state** on the Strap page, the notification and the widgets, with a Turn on button.
- Disconnect holds until you connect again or open the app. Background syncs no longer undo it.
- Synced only shows after a full sync, and the app and widgets agree on it.

## Smaller things

- Switching days shows the last day dimmed for a moment, then the new one, and the picked day is kept if Android closes the app.
- You and Strap no longer stack on top of each other.
- Location and notifications get a settings shortcut when they were turned down for good.
- Tapping a notification or widget doesn't repeat itself after Android closes the app.

## Upgrading

Installs over 3.0.4 and keeps all your data. Android 11 or newer.
